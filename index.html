How it works

Five fixed rules, evaluated on confirmed bar closes.

1 · Compression

Bollinger Bands (20, 2) inside a Keltner Channel (20, 1.5 ATR). The bands turn violet: get ready, no signal yet.

2 · Breakout + trend

A close above the upper band, with compression in the previous 5 bars and price above the 200 EMA, prints a cyan BUY.

3 · Known risk

Entry at the next open. Fixed stop at signal close − 1.5 × ATR(14). The label shows entry and stop for the latest trade only.

4 · Let it run

Exit when a bar closes back below the Bollinger basis. No small fixed targets: in testing they lost money after fees.

5 · Do not chase

Amber mark when price is already 1 ATR beyond the entry: a late entry would carry a much wider stop.

Size from the stop

The panel computes position size for a fixed % account risk and warns if your leverage puts liquidation before the stop.

FAQ

Does it repaint?

No. Signals, exits and alerts are decided on confirmed bar closes and never move afterwards. It uses no higher-timeframe requests.

Will it make me money?

No indicator can promise that and we won't. CL-01 gives you a rule-based plan and an honest record of how those rules behaved in the past, including the losing periods.

Which markets and timeframes?

Tested on crypto perpetuals (BTC, ETH and four other large caps) on 1H and 4H. It runs on any symbol, but below 1H the panel shows NOT RECOMMENDED because fees consumed the edge in testing.

How many signals will I get?

Few on 4H: about 2 per month on BTC in the backtest (152 trades in ~6.8 years). About 8 per month on 1H (650 trades). Compression alerts tell you when to look.

What about leverage?

Size positions from the stop and a fixed % of your account (the panel does the maths). High leverage can liquidate you before the stop is hit; the panel warns you when that is the case.
